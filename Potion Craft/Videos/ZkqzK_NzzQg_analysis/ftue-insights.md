# Potion Craft FTUE — Creator Insights (video ZkqzK_NzzQg, "Dr Incompetent" 2023 Beginner Guide Ep.1)

Commentary-narrated playthrough of the SAME v1.0 tutorial (new game → sleep → "end of tutorial"). Transcript-only pull (8,606 words). Value = the creator's *interpretation* of WHY each beat is taught and where new players get confused. Every claim below is `[genuine source: yt-02]` unless marked.

## Framing the creator gives the FTUE
- Pitches the game as **"relaxed, chill, no time crunch, not under the gun"** — repeats this ~4×. Says the no-timer reveal (customer waits forever) is *the* moment he "oohed and felt great about the game." FTUE's emotional hook = **safety / low-pressure**, not challenge.
- Explicitly says the tutorial is **incomplete**: "not everything is explained which is partially why I wanted to make this tutorial." Flags several systems the game teaches poorly (below).
- Story is delivered as intro text: found an abandoned wizard's house on edge of town, cleaned it up, opening a potion shop. Creator: "the game really isn't much about story but more about the business of exploring potions."

## Tutorial teach order (as narrated)
1. New game → prompt offers **Take Tutorial** (he takes it).
2. Intro story text → the shop screen ("get used to this screen").
3. First task: **grind/throw 2 Terraria + 2 Water Bloom into cauldron** (drag-and-drop, click-hold-release).
4. **Alchemy Map** scroll rolls down (projector-style) — clouded by fog of war; ingredients draw a path.
5. **Stir** with the ladle/spoon → potion travels the path; hit **experience book nodes** (+20 XP).
6. **Bellows** appear → pump to heat → reveals & brews **Weak Potion of Healing** (potency = Roman numeral, alignment-based).
7. **Finish Potion** button; potion effect Pips explained (up to 5 effects; potency adds hearts).
8. **Mortar & pestle** introduced → grinding *doubles potency* / halves ingredients.
9. **Save Recipe** into recipe book (starts with 12 pages).
10. **Move between rooms** (arrows / WASD) → go left to **Shop**.
11. **First customer** → "offer a potion, put it on the scale" → sell → gold + reputation + popularity.
12. **Haggling** mini-game introduced on 2nd customer.
13. **Lack of suitable potion** → the no-timer reveal → sent to **Enchanted Garden** (2 screens right) to gather ingredients.
14. Guided-but-freer brew: make a **Bottle of Poisoning** yourself ("try making a potion yourself," Try Again button offered).
15. Sell poison → reputation-morality note (pest poison = fine).
16. **Go to bed** (up to bedroom, click bed, confirm) → Moon passes → **Day 2** → "**this is the end of the tutorial.**"
17. Post-tutorial dump: ladle for water, goals/Alchemist Path book (ch.2 badge), talent points, garden-every-morning habit.

## Systems the creator says the tutorial teaches BADLY (new-player friction) — high-signal
- **Elemental compass / ingredient sort** (right panel): click a direction → auto-sorts ingredients by which travels furthest that way. Creator: *learned this from a viewer comment, not the game* — "incredibly powerful," was mousing over every ingredient before.
- **Haggle popularity cost:** the greyed "−1 star" means each haggle = one less popularity than a straight sale. "It shows you in the game but it's not quite as obvious."
- **Garden yields can be >1:** picking shows "+1" in the tutorial but live game can give e.g. 5 per node. "I thought it's just one of each" — explicit misconception called out.
- **Cauldron is irreversible:** once an ingredient's in the cauldron you cannot remove it; grind partially to control distance. Repeated 3×, clearly a common beginner mistake.
- **Grinding = 2× potency, half ingredients:** framed as the key efficiency lesson of the FTUE.
- **Potency = alignment:** the closer the bottle overlaps the map's potion icon, the higher the Roman numeral → more money. Level-1 heal sells low; level-3 would sell more.

## Creator's beginner advice woven through the FTUE
- **Prioritize buying ingredients** whenever on sale — "your lifeblood," repeated at start and end. Rebuilding the Alchemy machine / buying recipe pages can wait `[genuine source: yt-02]`.
- Early talent points: favor **trading (more money)** and **XP-book spawn**; map-visibility radius is "fine but not as important."
- **Gather the garden every morning** first thing (goal + XP); un-gathered plants are lost after sleep `[inferred by creator: "at least I think so"]`.
- Haggle difficulty (very easy / easy / medium) only changes needle SPEED and payout, not mechanics — start on very easy.

## FTUE end marker (confirmed by this source)
Going to bed on Day 1 → wake Day 2 → on-screen: *"you have refreshed your Alchemy knowledge and familiarized yourself with the Alchemy equipment… you found a few ingredients and a ladle."* Creator states plainly: **"this is the end of the tutorial… now the game just opens up."**

## Economy numbers stated
- First heal sale: **10 gold**, +2 reputation, +2 popularity (straight sale).
- Haggled sale: **+2 gold → 12 gold**, but 1 popularity instead of 2.
- Rat-poison sale: **13 gold**.
- Recipe book: **12 pages** to start; 1 per saved recipe, erasable.
- Reaches **level 3** with 2 talent points by tutorial end.
