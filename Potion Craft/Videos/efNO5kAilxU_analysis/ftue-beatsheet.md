## 5. Friction & notable UX

- **Longest dwell of the whole session:** f89-99 (2:56-3:16, ~22 seconds / 6 frames of visually near-identical "Brew a Potion of Poisoning" screen) while the player grinds Terraria, then Firebell, then assembles the cauldron - this is the first "solo" brew with no step-by-step hand-holding (contrast with the earlier fully-scripted Healing potion), and it visibly takes the player longer to work through even though the ingredients were already told to them.
- The "Brew a Potion of Poisoning" panel keeps a persistent **"Try again"** reset button visible the entire time (f89-101), signaling the designers expect/allow failed attempts here - no failed attempt was captured in this run, but the button's constant presence is a deliberate low-stakes safety net.
- The "Lack of Suitable Potions" tutorial (f80, ~2:38) explicitly reassures the player there is **no time limit** and **the client will not leave** - a proactive friction-reduction message anticipating player anxiety about being unprepared for a customer's specific request.
- Two of three customer interactions escalate into the haggle mini-game; haggling is only explicitly tutorialized once (f70-77, customer 2) and then reused wordlessly for customer 3 (f107-110), testing whether the player retained the mechanic.
- The stirring/heating/finishing sequence is taught twice back-to-back for the same potion type (first with 2+2 ingredients at 0:20-0:52, then repeated with the ground-down 1+1 version at 1:06-1:40) - a deliberate repetition-for-reinforcement pattern rather than friction, but it does add ~1 extra minute before the player ever leaves the lab.
- Customer portraits are distinct per visit (bonneted woman, hooded man, loose-haired woman) - no repeated art noticed in this early window.
