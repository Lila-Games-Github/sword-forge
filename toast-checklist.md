# Toast checklist (landscape build, 2026-11-05)

Tick `[x]` on each toast to delete. Text in `<...>` is filled in by the game. A † marks a toast that shows an
em-dash in the game; it is written here as " - ".

## Counter and customers

- [ ] "Someone is already at the counter" - you ring the bell for the next tutorial-part customer while a customer is still at the counter.
- [ ] "Someone is already at the counter" - a new customer is called while one is still at the counter (normal play).
- [x] "Handed over. You are paid when the whole order is sold." - you give one sword of a multi-sword order; gold comes only with the last sword.
- [ ] "Put a sword on the counter first" - you tap SELL with no sword on the counter.
- [ ] "Bram asked for a **Swift Dagger**" - Bram's second visit: you try to sell him a sword that is not a dagger.
- [ ] "They asked for **<order list>**" - the sword on the counter is not one of the swords the customer's order still needs.
- [ ] "They asked for a **<tier> <sword>**" - the sword on the counter does not match the customer's order.
- [x] "He took it and paid **nothing**" - you sell Garric a sword of the lowest grade after he asked for a better one.
- [x] "Sold for **<g>g**" / "Sold <n> swords for **<g>g**", plus "· +<n> popularity" - a counter sale completes.
- [x] "⭐ Popularity level <n> - swords now fetch <n>% more" † - a sale raises your popularity level.
- [x] "⚔️ <shape> on the counter - <price>g" † - you drop a sword on the counter.
- [x] "Back in your inventory" - you take a sword back off the counter.
- [x] "Not yet. The dragon will tell you when." - Part 1, before Bram's sale: you drag a sword onto the counter too early.
- [ ] "They will wait." - you tap COME LATER on a customer.
