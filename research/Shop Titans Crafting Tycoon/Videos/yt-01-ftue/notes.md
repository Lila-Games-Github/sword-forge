## Core loop actions

1. Customize/create shopkeeper avatar -> enter shop.
2. Talk to mentor NPC (Wallace) who narrates early actions (storage, equipping items).
3. Craft an item at the workbench (green "Craft" button; a queued item shows a countdown timer, e.g. "3s"/"2s").
4. Sell/give the crafted item to a customer NPC standing in the shop (Julia reacts to item rarity/quality).
5. Earn gold (and occasionally gems) from the sale; watch for "MASTERPIECE" crit-craft outcomes with an upsell to a higher rarity.
6. Recruit a hero via "Hero Creation" (choose name + class), then equip found items (e.g. a legendary item) on that hero.
7. Progress toward level-2 threshold via a "Sell items to reach level 2" quest banner.

- Player stands at a workbench inside the shop plot; tapping "Craft" starts a timed crafting action (observed countdown "3s" -> "2s" on the Craft button while an item icon, e.g. a sword, appears queued above/near it).

## Progression pacing (levels, unlocks, gating, time/wait mechanics)

- Player shop level rises from 1 to 3 within the ~12-minute recorded session (fast early pacing, consistent with FTUE design meant to hook players quickly).
- A time-gated reward icon ("10:23h" countdown) appears on the left HUD from early on, introducing the wait-timer/return-later mechanic within the first few minutes, well before it would organically matter.
- Crafting itself uses a short real-time countdown (observed 2-3 seconds) at this early stage, which is trivial at level 1 but foreshadows the wait-to-craft mechanic that presumably scales up.
- The "Sell items to reach level 2" banner is a single, clear, persistent objective - the FTUE keeps exactly one goal visible at a time rather than a full quest log.

## 5 key findings

- The FTUE opens with an unusually deep avatar customization screen (body/hair/face/clothes tabs, sliders, palette picker) before any gameplay - it front-loads personalization/identity investment ahead of the core craft-sell loop, a retention lever distinct from Sword Forge's more utilitarian setup flow.
- Mentor NPC (Wallace) narrates single, atomic next-actions in short banked dialogue lines ("that item went to storage," "equip that legendary item you just found") rather than a modal tutorial overlay - the tutorial rides on ordinary NPC dialogue UI, keeping the player inside diegetic shop-floor space at all times.
- The crit-craft path (regular result vs. "MASTERPIECE!" outstanding item) is paired immediately with a one-tap "Upgrade to EPIC - Free" button on the same popup - the rarity-upgrade interaction pattern is taught for free in the FTUE window, priming players to recognize (and later pay for) the same button once it costs gems.
- Two distinct "create a person" flows exist back to back: shopkeeper customization at game start, then a separate Hero Creation (name + class + stat-star preview) flow mid-session for recruiting combat heroes - the game teaches character-investment mechanics twice, reinforcing collection/roster meta early.
- Time-gated systems (a countdown reward icon, short craft-timers) are surfaced within the first 10 minutes even though they are inert at this stage (10:23h reward, 2-3s craft) - the FTUE seeds the wait-mechanic UI early so it reads as familiar once timers become meaningful (i.e., long) later in progression.
