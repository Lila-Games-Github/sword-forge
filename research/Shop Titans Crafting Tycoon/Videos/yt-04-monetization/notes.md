## Progression pacing (how paying accelerates progression)

- Shop expansion normally gated behind both a player level (50/60/75) AND a multi-day real-time timer (1d23h / 2d23h / 4d23h) even once the level is hit — gems bypass both simultaneously.
- Crafting slots are nominally available via gold + leveling alone (no timer shown), but host argues the compounding value of extra concurrent crafts (30+ minute craft times at his tier) makes buying them early with gems worth more than the raw gold-equivalent, i.e. the monetization pitch shifts from "skip a wait" to "compound a rate" once timers aren't in play.
- Chests convert gems into a guaranteed-rare loot floor, letting the player skip repeating early "zone 1" quest content entirely for the specific mats (research scrolls, ascension shards) that gate hero/gear progression — this is a pay-to-skip-content lever, not just pay-to-skip-time.
