## https://st-central.net/beginners-corner/

Comprehensive beginner resource. Organized into several sections:

**Main Guides:** Three-part progression guides covering levels 1-40, 40-53, and 53+, plus introductory material on heroes and champions.

**Key Unlocking Mechanics:**
- Market Access: Equipment tier availability increases with merchant level (Tier 2 at level 7, advancing to Tier 15 at level 65).
- Hero Equipment: Heroes unlock equipment tiers based on personal level (Tier 2 at level 3, up to Tier 15 at level 38).
- Uncommon Resources: Players reach level 7 in common buildings first, then place corresponding resource bins for Steel, Hardwood, Fabric, and Oil.

**Resource Management:**
- "the best use for Ascension Shards initially is to work on ascending all of your item lines to 15 stars" for a 10% cost reduction.
- For Seeds, prioritize Champions first, particularly Argon, with up to 40 of each seed type usable per hero.

**Important Restrictions:**
- Players cannot maintain multiple accounts — violations result in permanent leaderboard and market bans.
- All purchases are non-refundable; attempting chargebacks triggers automatic market bans.

**Terminology Reference:** A glossary of 20+ game abbreviations and mechanical terms, from BiS (Best in Slot) to Weapon Skills.

## https://st-central.net/heroes-and-champions/

Beginner's guide explaining heroes and champions: types, stats, skills, and the special permanent champions.

**Hero Types:**
- Fighters (Red Types): "Fighters primary purpose in your teams is to act as a tank – they aim to take the majority of the damage allowing your squishier heroes to attack in peace. They achieve this with the use of threat and having high defence and health."
- Rogues (Green Types): "Rogues bring burst damage to your party. They tend to rely on critical strikes to deliver devastating amounts of damage, whilst using evasion to dodge any hits aimed in their direction."
- Spellcasters (Blue Types): "Spellcasters bring reliable damage and are the least likely of the 3 types to be hit by the enemy."

**Core Stats:**
- HP: Damage a hero can receive before falling; dead heroes gain no XP and face increased recovery time
- Attack: Damage dealt to enemies
- Critical Hit Chance: Probability of multiplying attack damage
- Defence: Reduces enemy damage taken
- Evasion: Chance to avoid being hit (hard cap: 75%)
- Threat: Determines likelihood of enemy targeting
- Elements: Affect skill power when gear is enchanted

**Champions:** 11 permanent heroes with special powers that upgrade via hero coins: Argon, Lilu, Sia, Yami, Rudo, Polonia, Donovan, Hemma, Ashley, Bjorn, and Malady. "Should a Champion be killed in battle, their power will still remain active."

## https://st-central.net/guild-perks/

**Guild-Based Perks:**
- Guild Level controls access to perks.
- Guild's Choice Cooldown reduces targeting investment wait times.
- Bounty Board Capacity increases available bounties for members.
- Resource Package Stack allows up to +3 additional daily guild coin renewals (3 levels maximum).

**Furniture-Based Perks:**
- Item Racks boost energy from respective furniture types and should be prioritized.
- Resource Bins vary in effectiveness — uncommon and rare versions provide smaller increases.
- Storage perks increase component yields but aren't urgent upgrades.

**Boosts (4 Types):**
- Resource Boost is "king" among boosts, providing 50% regeneration increase at maximum rank.
- Quest Boost accelerates hero questing and healing.
- Crafting Boost reduces crafting times, becoming stronger in later tiers.
- Experience Boost increases hero, worker, and merchant XP gains.

**Additional Perks:**
- Trade Perks offer +3 to +30 market capacity bonuses but require steep costs.
- Permanent Regen Boosts range from +2% to +20% resource regeneration across five levels with significant renown investment.

Recommendation: prioritize perks over boosts for low-activity guilds, focusing first on Resource and Quest boosts for active ones.

## https://st-central.net/tips/

- Customer Coverage: Players can serve all customer types using just three item types: Clothes, Light Armor, and Heavy Gauntlets. These items all occupy mannequin racks, streamlining inventory management.
- Grab Bags: Guild grab bags have varying contents based on color and dungeon area. "Area refers to the order you unlock resources in a dungeon. Using Howling Woods as an example: Elven Wood is area 1, Iron Pine Cone is area 2, Glow Shroom is area 3." Sunken areas function differently.
- Rounding Mechanics: The game applies aggressive rounding for items valued above 1 million gold, which players can exploit to generate additional currency.
- Market Restocking: Placing a rack in storage then retrieving it automatically restocks it with recently purchased market items, eliminating manual restocking work.
- Champion Requests: Two strategies when champions request gem-exclusive items: submit a market buy request at maximum gold, or fulfill requests using superior/flawless quality alternatives rather than common items.
- Market Storage: Inventory overflow can be managed using market slots to store up to 50 components or 25 items temporarily.
- Hero Gear Table: A comprehensive reference chart displays all hero classes with compatible gear options.

## https://st-central.net/hero-academia/

**Core Hero Types:**
- Red Type (Fighters/Tanks): High HP and defense with lower damage output. Examples: Soldiers, Barbarians, Knights, Rangers, Samurai. Strategy focuses on absorbing damage while contributing to offense.
- Green Type (Rogues): Rely on evasion or critical hits. Classes: Thieves, Monks, Musketeers, Wanderers, Ninjas, Dancers. Require careful gearing but deal significant damage through precision.
- Blue Type (Spellcasters): Highest base attack values. Classes: Mages, Clerics, Druids, Sorcerers, Spellblades, Geomancers, Chronomancers. Provide consistent firepower with lower threat ratings.

**Key Mechanics:**
- Seeds and Blossoms: Heroes can receive up to 40 seeds each (Attack +4, Health +1, Defense +4), expandable to 80 with Blossoms. Priority goes to Champions first, then promoted heroes.
- Quest Boosters: Three types — Compasses (reduce time/heal), Experience Boosters, and Power Boosters (+20-80% ATK/DEF depending on tier).
- Elemental Barriers: Starting at Cinderlake Volcano, bosses have barriers reducing damage by 80% until broken. Barrier health must be exceeded by party elemental value. Spellblades deal 50% effectiveness against any barrier.
- Extreme Difficulty: Unlocks at level 40, applies -20% evasion penalty and includes elemental barriers.

**Foundational Principle:** "Build rosters, not heroes" — develop diverse teams capable of clearing all content before pursuing perfect individual heroes.

## https://st-central.net/merchant-academia/

**Core Gameplay Loop** — five essential activities:
1. Crafting — "Your craft slots should always be full and spinning"
2. Upgrading — Always have furniture upgrading
3. Rushing — Use energy to refill slots as resources permit
4. Selling — Avoid maintaining a full counter during active play
5. Questing — Keep quest slots filled with heroes

**Choosing Crafts:** "No one size fits all pattern is perfect for everyone." Success requires finding items that "maximize profit per craft slot, utilizing all resources with no overlap"; crafting mastered, 3-star ascended items; rushing with energy immediately when resources refill; calculating profitability by determining crafts per hour, expenses, and revenue. The strategy involves identifying top earners for rare resources (jewels/ether) separately from basic resources.

**Customer Flow** — three factors determine customer availability:
- Shop size — Max 12 customers across 9 expansions
- Counter level — 5 customers at levels 1-5; 8 at levels 6-10; 12 at level 11+
- Display stock — Aim for 90-105 rack items maximum

These factors are interdependent; "if one of them is lower than the other 2...it will limit them."

**No Bin Strategy** (advanced): eliminates resource bins, replacing them with racks. Requirements: "4200+ Max Energy"; all items at x1.25 gold value milestone; heroes capable of efficient farming. Involves buying market items to surcharge in-shop. A practitioner reports "70% better" profits than optimal crafting, with potential earnings of "300m on a bad day...750m on a good day."

**Fusion Mechanics:** merges lower-quality items into higher tiers.
- Success rates: Superior/Flawless/Epic: 100%; Legendary: 50%
- Requires lower-quality items and moonstones. "Because fusion does not use resources, it is not possible to rush these crafts with energy – you can only use gems."
- Full Moon Fusion allows converting items like chests into components and occurs on real-life full moons for 24 hours.

**Quality & Crafting Odds (approximate):**

| Quality | Chance |
|---------|--------|
| Normal | 95.041% |
| Superior | 3.971% |
| Flawless | 0.669% |
| Epic | 0.269% |
| Legendary | 0.05% |

Value multipliers: Superior (1.25x), Flawless (2x), Epic (3x), Legendary (5x).

**Multicraft:** produces two items instead of one. Modifiers are additive — ascension grants 10%, reaching 21 stars adds 5%, and events can add 25% for a potential 40% combined chance.

**Rounding Mechanics:** rounds values to nearest amounts based on item worth. Above 1,000,000 gold, values round to nearest 50,000. Strategic enchanting placement can create "free money" through favorable rounding, with specialized calculators available to optimize combinations.

**Small Talk:** Success rate approximately 75%. Energy calculations:
- Success: "(Max Energy – Current Energy) × 0.1"
- Failure: "Max Energy × 0.07"
Recommendations: small talk until reaching 50-75% max energy and attempt after surcharging but before completing sales.

## https://st-central.net/gaining-merchant-xp-efficiently/

**XP Sources** — merchant XP comes from four sources:
1. "Selling items to customers in your shop (NOT affected by surcharge/discounts)"
2. Sales to the king
3. Supplying the ship during dragon invasion events
4. Overflowing items with the Roadside Wholesale talent (grants 50% XP)

**XP Calculation Formula:** "Base XP * (1 + Evelyn) * (1 + Guild Boost) * (1 + Advanced Research) * Rarity Multiplier * King Multiplier". Each item has independent XP values unaffected by pricing adjustments.

**Leveling Methods:**
- Cheap Method: Purchase budget equipment from the market and stock your shop quickly for quantity-focused sales.
- Efficient Method: Prioritize premium item lines with higher base XP. Top items include Aurasongs, Wands/Crossbows, and Dual Wields. Recommends "a food only rack because this will increase the number of sales you can do per hour."
- Highest XP Method: Fill your shop with top-tier items, use double enchantments, aim for superior+ rarity, and leverage overflow mechanics with Roadside Wholesale while maintaining shop sales.

**Advanced Research Impact:** "going from no AR to having half of all lines unlocked up to XP is very impactful" — a 180% increase versus only 64% additional gain beyond that point.

## https://st-central.net/fun-with-furniture/

**Bin to Rack Ratio Strategy** — depends on playstyle:
- For Casual Players (logging in every few hours): Prioritize bins to fill all crafting slots. "fewer high-level bins than to have many low-level bins if both end up having the same capacity."
- For Active Players: Minimize bins while maintaining at least 25% maximum resource capacity. This threshold prevents triggering worker refills prematurely — crucial when crafting Tier 8+ items requiring Tier 3 resources (Ether and Jewels). "Tier 1 resources (Iron, Wood, Herb, Leather) and even the tier 2 resources...regenerate quickly" while Essence becomes "the biggest bottlenecks for late-game higher tier crafts."

**Recommended Late-Game Configuration:**
- One bin per Tier 1 resource type
- One bin per Tier 2 resource type
- Two bins per Tier 3 resource type
- Three to five Essence Beacons

**Dragonmark Upgrade Priority** (Dragonmarks are limited currency from the Dragon Invasion event):
1. Dragon Hoard to Level 16 — unlocks resource tiers and benefits from guild perks
2. Counter to Level 25 — provides 1% energy boost per level, capping at 10%
3. Vending Machine to Level 22 — offers superior energy efficiency
4. Racks progressively — alternating with Vending Machine upgrades afterward

The guide provides specific Dragonmark-per-energy calculations to justify this sequence.

## https://st-central.net/ascensions/

**Core Recommendation:** prioritize Surcharge Energy Cost Reduction at 15 ascension stars for every item line before specializing. This milestone reduces surcharge energy costs by 10% across all items in that track.

**Energy Savings Examples** (per surcharge):
- Tier 3: "Saving: 7"
- Tier 6: "Saving: 27"
- Tier 9: "Saving: 89"
- Tier 10: "Saving: 179-290" depending on item rarity
- Tier 15: "Saving: 782"

**Strategic Advantages of reaching 15 stars in all lines first:**
1. Universal application — reduction applies regardless of acquisition method (crafting, market purchase, quest drops, or workers)
2. Future-proofing — avoids specializing in lines that might become obsolete with new content
3. Cost-efficiency — ascending lower-tier items to reach 15 stars costs approximately 165 shards per line, less expensive than full specialization

**Secondary Goals** (after 15 in all lines): focus on Elements and Spirits for gem value, ascend select lines to 51 for titan bonuses, or reach 21 in all lines for multicraft bonuses.

## https://st-central.net/tower-of-titans/

**Event Overview:** "Tower of Titans is a long series of quests to challenge player's rosters, in the form of floors within a tower." Runs monthly from the 2nd to the 30th at 15:00 UTC and operates independently without guild assistance.

**Core Mechanics:**
- Six difficulty tiers: Alpha (level 20+), Beta (35+), Gamma (45+), Delta (58+), Epsilon (67+), and Terror (67+)
- Structure: 30 floors total with boss encounters every third floor
- Team composition: 4 participants maximum per fight
- Timing: 1-hour quest duration with 2-hour rest periods

**Boss Modifiers** (randomly assigned):
- "Can Opener" — targets fighters while reducing their defense by 30%
- "Magic Devourer" — increases threat toward spellcasters and reduces their damage output by 20%
- "Ambusher" — denies evasion entirely for the first three rounds

**Unique Rewards:**
- Skill Reroll Dice — four tiers by skill slot: Basic, Super, Mega, and Perfect variants.
- Titan's Soul — awarded upon tower completion as a single-use promotion item enabling hero advancement to exclusive Titan classes.
- Standard rewards include ascension shards, research scrolls, and champion coins across all difficulties.

Events organized into three categories:

**General Events:** "seemingly random occurrences that do not fit into the other categories that affect specific game mechanics." Full Moon events provide access to fusion recipes during real-world full moons.

**Guild Events:**
- Lost City of Gold (Merchant level 25+): Players "plunder the ruins of an ancient civilization for gold bars" to trade for rewards
- The King's Caprice (Merchant level 25+): Guilds craft weapons and armor collectively
- Dragon Invasion (Merchant level 25+): A defensive event where players "defend the city against a hoard of dragons"

**Solo Events:**
- Tower of Titans (Merchant level 20+): "ascend a tower of ever increasing difficulty to gather fame, fortune and a Titan Soul"
