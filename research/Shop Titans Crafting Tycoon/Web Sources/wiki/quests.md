'''Quests''' are a core mechanic where [[Champions]] and [[Heroes]] are sent to [[Dungeons]] to gain [[Components#Quest Components|components]] and other [[items]]. Through quests, [[experience]] is earned to level up Champions and Heroes.

Leveling up Champions and Heroes increases their [[Quest Statistics|questing ability]], allowing them to do harder quests.

== Quest Overview ==

There are different quests that progressively become harder as the player improves his/her [[Champions]] and [[Heroes]]. By completing a quest a certain number of times, the next quest becomes available and you can unlock medium & hard mode. The following is a list of the quests:
