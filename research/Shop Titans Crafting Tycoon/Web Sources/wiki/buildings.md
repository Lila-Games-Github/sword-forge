# Buildings

Source: https://shop-titans.fandom.com/wiki/Buildings

Buildings are located within the City and it is how a Guild can improve their overall game experience. They can be broken down into three types: Workers , Resources , and Special . 

## Contents 
- 1 Workers 
- 2 Resources 
- 3 Special 

## Workers [ ] 
Workers serve two functions for players as they level up, they cap which level Items that you can make and improve the speed of crafting those items. 

## Resources [ ] 
Resources buildings improve the speed to gather Resources . 

## Special [ ] 
Special buildings provide different benefits to the Guild .
