# Barren Wastes

Source: https://shop-titans.fandom.com/wiki/Barren_Wastes

## Barren Wastes 

### Maximum Party Size 
3 

## Rewards 

### Chest and Key 
Limestone Chest 

### Component Rewards 
White Sand 

Bronze Fang 
Moon Crystal 

## Unlock 

The Barren Wastes is a dungeon where the shopkeeper can send champions and heroes on quests . 

## Contents 
- 1 Requirements and XP 
- 2 Times 
- 3 Component and Item Rewards 
- 4 Monster Statistics 
- 5 Progression 

## Requirements and XP [ ] 
When sending champions and heroes , they must have a power equal to or greater than the minimum requirement and those sent who are not wounded will earn the experience . 

## Times [ ] 
Questing and resting take a certain amount of time and if your hero or champion is downed, it takes more time to rest. 

## Component and Item Rewards [ ] 
By completing quests in the Barren Wastes, certain quest components and other random items can be earned. 

## Monster Statistics [ ] 
For a better understanding of how these statistics work, please look at Quest Statistics . 

## Progression [ ] 
Progression of quests comes by completing them successfully a certain number of times.
