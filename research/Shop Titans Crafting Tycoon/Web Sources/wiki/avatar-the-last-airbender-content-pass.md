# Avatar: The Last Airbender Content Pass

Source: https://shop-titans.fandom.com/wiki/Avatar%3A_The_Last_Airbender_Content_Pass

## Avatar: The Last Airbender Content Pass 

## Requirements 

### Release Date 
November 1, 2021 

### Full Access Price 
1,500 

## Rewards 

### Event Currency 
Copper Coins 

Available during November 2021, the content pass provided customizations themed around Aang’s gang, decorations, an exclusive pet (Momo!) and even some blueprints. Complete tasks to earn points to unlock these items! 

## Contents 
- 1 Rewards 
- 2 Full Access 
- 3 Golden Tasks 
- 4 Guest Champions 
- 4.1 Aang, the Last Airbender 
- 4.2 Sokka, Water Tribe Warrior 
- 5 New Pet: Momo 

## Rewards [ ] 

## Full Access [ ] 
The Full Access section of the Content Pass is available exclusively for gems. Once purchased, its rewards become available for players with enough points. 

## Golden Tasks [ ] 
Similar to bounties, you may notice a few golden tasks at the bottom of the list. These require some form of premium activity to complete (e.g crafting premium item lines, spending gems, being Royal Merchant, etc). 
Despite this, regular tasks from the content pass provide enough points to reach the final milestone with some points to spare. 

## Guest Champions [ ] 
Guest Champions are a new category of Champions with a few special rules. Their most outstanding feature is that they are only available for a limited time. Once their time is up, they will leave the player’s roster. 

### Aang, the Last Airbender [ ] 
Hailing from distant lands... the Avatar has arrived! While his flying bison is resting, Aang will be happy to assist shopkeepers by going on quests as a Champion! 
As a party leader, Aang will greatly increase his group’s Attack and Defense. In addition, Aang will also find bonus loot in the form of Elements! 
Aang will visit player shops every day for a month, requesting various items in exchange for 5 Champion Coins. Check in daily! 

### Sokka, Water Tribe Warrior [ ] 
Tagging alongside Aang, Sokka also joins the fray! Welcome to Team Avatar, shopkeeper! 
As a party leader, Sokka is a versatile combatant that provides bonuses to his party’s ATK and HP. In addition, he also increases the quantity of components found AND XP earned by the party! 
Unlike Aang, Sokka will only start visiting the player’s shop once they have earned some of his coins from the Content Pass. Once he does, he will visit daily, granting 5 Champion Coins for fulfilling his request. 

## New Pet: Momo [ ] 
A new pet, Momo the Winged Lemur, is now available for a limited time! 
Momo is exclusive to the Content Pass, so if you’d like to have Aang’s best buddy hang around your shop, don’t miss it! Unlike Guest Champions, Momo can be kept forever once unlocked.
