[[File:Classes.png|thumb]]

Heroes are player-created characters that can be used to send on Quests and gather materials.

Currently, there are 3 class archetypes (Fighter, Rogue, and Spellcaster) with 6 sub-classes for a total of 18 classes available in Shop Titans.
