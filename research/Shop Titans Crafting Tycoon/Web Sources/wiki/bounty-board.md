# Bounty Board

Source: https://shop-titans.fandom.com/wiki/Bounty_Board

The bounty board is a weekly guild event . 

## Overview [ ] 
The bounty board allows the guild to earn renown for Guild Perks and Guild Boosts . Guild members can take a bounty , at which point they have 24 hours to complete it. As a reward, the guild gains 1-3 trophies , which are exchanged for renown at the weekly reset (as shown in the table below). The guild member also receives 5-25 guild coins .
