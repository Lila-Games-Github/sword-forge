# Bosses

Source: https://shop-titans.fandom.com/wiki/Bosses

Bosses are a enemies in Shop Titans . Each quest has a boss that appears at timed intervals. In general they have more hit points, hit harder and are tougher than your average enemy, but their fights produce better rewards. 

Lost City of Gold events have Keykeeper golem bosses. At each stage they get harder. You cannot set the toughness and can not revert to do an easier level. Max 12 Keykeeper battles per LCoG event. 
Tower of Titan bosses are every 3 levels. They have special upgrades and so require some strategy to beat. Be prepared to mix up your teams and champions to get best results.
