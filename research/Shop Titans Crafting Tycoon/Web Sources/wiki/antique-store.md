# Antique Store

Source: https://shop-titans.fandom.com/wiki/Antique_Store

The Antique Store is a special store which is only active during the King’s Caprice event. The shopkeeper can purchase select content from past seasonal offers with Antique Tokens . 

## Antique Store Rules [ ] 
- Seasonal Offer content (packs sold by NPCs) must be at least 6 months old before it can appear in the Antique Store. 
- Starter Pack content (currently only includes the Kunai) requires the player’s account to be at least 3 months old before it can appear in the Antique Store. 
- Content from various other sources, such as Premium Worker blueprints , seasonal event content or limited-time giveaways will not be featured in the Antique Store.
