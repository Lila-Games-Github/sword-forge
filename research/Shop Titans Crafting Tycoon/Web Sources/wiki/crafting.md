'''Crafting''' is a primary mechanic in [[Shop Titans]] where the player can create [[Items|items]] from [[Blueprints|blueprints]] to sell within their [[Shop]].

==Crafting Slots==

The number of [[Items|items]] that a player can craft at one time is limited by the number of '''Crafting Slots''' they have unlocked. These Crafting Slots can be purchased with [[gold]] after the player attains a certain [[Shopkeeper#Merchant Level|merchant level]]. Alternatively, the merchant level requirement can be bypassed by purchasing with [[gems]].
