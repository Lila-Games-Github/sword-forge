# Champions

Source: https://shop-titans.fandom.com/wiki/Champions

Champions are characters that can be sent on quests to lead a party of heroes . Only one champion can be sent with a party and each one provides a different leadership skill and other boosts to improve party stats . Unlike other heroes, champions are not equipped with gear. Champions, with the exception of Argon , are bought with Champion Coins which can also be used to progress the champion.
