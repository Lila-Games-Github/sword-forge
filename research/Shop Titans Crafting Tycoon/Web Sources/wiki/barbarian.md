## Strategy [ ] 
Consider focusing your Barbarian on defensive skills, like Thoughness, On Guard or Tough Skin and enchant their equipments with Bull or Ram spirit, or even Mammoth if you have some. Personally, I do like the Lizard one too, because of the healing it gives. With enough Threat (and luck lmao), you Barbarian can survive to really long fights without Lilu 's help. 
A good skill on the Barbarians, like with other Fighters, is Maintenance, because even if it can look like trash, this skill is very effective on defensive character, because it gives +10% to +20% (or +20% to +40%) bonus stats to each item your character got. So it's pretty much like +20% defense, regarding the fact that Fighters have almost only armor/defensive stuff (like potions or herbs ), and less breaking chances are always a good thing to have.
