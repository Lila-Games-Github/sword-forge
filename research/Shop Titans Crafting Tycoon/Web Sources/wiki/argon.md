- Argon : " ...And that's when I slashed the Troublin with my sword! Pow!"

- Argon : " Don't mention it! If I am to become a knight someday, I must help people in need!" 

- Elder Owen : " Just like your father, eh? I'm sure you'll make a fine knight, my boy. Why perhaps you could even work as the King's royal guard! Speak of the devil, there he is at the market! Why not go ask him?"

#### Side Story - The Tower of Titans [ ] 
- Elder Owen: "The Tower of Titans, eh?" 
- Lilu: "Please tell us all about it! 
- Argon: "I herd some Heroes whisper about it, but no one has ever seen it..." 
- Elder Owen: "Indeed, The Tower of Titans is a mythical place. Nobody really knows if it truly exists. Stories say it appears out of thin air, challenging the mightiest Heroes to climb its many floors... Before suddenly disappearing, wiping clan the memories of anyone who may have seen it." 
- Argon: "W-wiping memories!?" 
- Lilu: "That's strange... where would the legend come from, then?" 
- Argon: "Right! some people would need to remember!" 
- Elder Owen: "It is said that those who overcome the titan's challenges are deemed worthy by the Tower. These Heroes return... changed from the experience, and retain their memories. More accurately, a part of them remains in the Tower, and a part of the Tower remains with them... That will be all for today's story time. Thank you for listening to this old man's tales. 
- Lilu: "Wow! I wonder if it truly exists?" 
- Argon: "Yeah! I'd love to explore a dungeon like that!" 
- Lilu: "But one thing seems strange... How does Elder Owen know all this?" 
- Argon: "...!"

### Chapter 6 - Second Light [ ] 
- Sia: "And so, that... "Knight", had your assassin at his mercy... Yet decided to let her go, believing her to be a good person!" 
- Reinhold: "A fascinating tale! This new knight appears to be a promising fellow!" 
- Sia: "With all due respect, my King... This man failed to stop your assassin, on account of a mere gut feeling." 
- Reinhold: "The hallmark of a Paladin, wouldn't you think?" 
- Sia: "Excuse me?" 
- Reinhold: "To have your Nemesis at your mercy, yet seeing beyond mere strife and conflict... Then letting them go, so certain of their redemption that you are willing to put your title on the line. A tremendous act of bravery and benevolence!" 
- Sia: "I... must admit I did not consider this perspective, my King. But the kingdom has not seen an aspiring Paladin in decades... Shall I summon the knight council to debate the matter?" 
- Reinhold: "Nonsense! I shall handle the formalities myself." 
- Sia: "As you wish, my King. I shall prepare an escort immediately." 
- Reinhold: "There is no need. I shall be as discreet as a mouse!"
