# Champion Coins

Source: https://shop-titans.fandom.com/wiki/Champion_Coins

Each champion has a personalized Champion Coin which allows them to progress. These coins can be obtained from chests , quests , Fortune Zone Prizes , Guildmate Gifts , Piggy Bank , Guild Coin Bag , and Champion Request . 

### Champion Requests [ ] 
Every day, 2-4 champions will make a request for a certain quantity of specific items . In return for filling his/her request, the player will receive Champion Coins of the requesting champion. 

An independent source has a calendar that displays champion rotations in real-time.
