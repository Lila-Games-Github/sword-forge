# 2020 Anniversary

Source: https://shop-titans.fandom.com/wiki/2020_Anniversary

For the 2020 Anniversary , Kabam Games, Inc. decided to hold a month of events for their first anniversary of Shop Titans .  Many of the events were post on the Shop Titans Twitter .  It also included the giveaway of multiple Shop Titan Tees and, at the time, unknown Pet-Packs .
