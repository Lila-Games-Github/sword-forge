## Eight Tips for Beginners

**1. Trust the Tutorial When You Start Out**
The tutorial is helpful and doesn't aggressively push spending. It teaches core mechanics: running the shop, creating items, obtaining characters, and developing your business.

**2. Achieve a Balance Between Surcharges and Discounts**
Players should understand Energy management. Surcharging increases prices but decreases Energy, while Discounts reduce prices but restore Energy. The strategy involves "Surcharge on expensive items, while offering Discounts for cheaper ones."

**3. Join a Guild as Soon as You're Ready**
Guilds provide upgrades, gifts, Energy boosts, and milestone rewards. The article recommends exploring Reddit and Discord communities before joining, as "The best guilds will usually advertise open spots there."

**4. Decorate Your Shop for Additional Energy**
Furniture adds Prestige and allows customers to interact with items, generating Energy. Decoration is optional for competitive play but beneficial for casual players.

**5. Create Items to Unlock Better Ones**
Players unlock better recipes by using available patterns repeatedly. Chests offer alternatives but are less F2P-friendly.

**6. Upgrade Your Displays for More Energy**
Weapon racks and armor mannequins can be upgraded to hold more items and increase maximum Energy. Counter upgrades increase Energy gained per sale.

**7. Send Heroes on Quests**
Champions and heroes should be regularly sent on quests for materials, chests, and special items. The tutorial guides initial quests; players must continue independently afterward.

**8. Be Frugal with Your Diamonds**
Diamonds (premium currency) should be hoarded. Avoid upgrading items or resting heroes with diamonds. Instead, save for skill re-rolls, shop expansion, or crafting slots.
