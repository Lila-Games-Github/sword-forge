## Overview

A comprehensive ~6,000-word guide to Shop Titans, a mobile/PC tycoon game by Kabam. The author actively plays but strongly advises avoiding the game due to its monetization model and time-intensive nature.

## Core Warnings

Main thesis: "Seriously, avoid this game! And any game monetized like it."

While technically "free to play," the game functions as a sophisticated monetization trap with exponential costs. Genuine progression requires either massive time investment (hundreds/thousands of hours) or substantial real money spending ($10,000+).

## Key Game Mechanics

**Shop Management:**
- Craft items and sell them for gold and energy
- Energy generation tied to surcharges and shop upgrades
- Limited to one account per player (multiple accounts result in bans)
- No resets or do-overs available

**Progression Systems:**
- Merchant levels (reaching level 99-100+ requires billions of experience points)
- Town hall and building upgrades (costs grow exponentially)
- City investments measured in trillions of gold for top guilds
- Guild-dependent gameplay requiring 20+ active members

**Energy Mechanics:**
- Sales counter (15 levels): generates 1-30 energy per sale
- Display furniture: determines maximum energy capacity
- Surcharges: primary energy source
- Energy caps at high levels create progression walls

## Monetization Breakdown

**Premium Workers (One-Time Purchases):**
- Mundra Moon Dragon: $17.99
- Wise Scholar: $19.99
- Crafty Engineer: $29.99
- Summoner: $20.00

**Ongoing Costs:**
- Royal Merchant subscription: $9.99/month
- Event packs: ~$20 each (seasonal: Halloween, Christmas, etc.)
- Gems: $99.99 for large sacks; used for instant upgrades

**Ascension Shards:** Achieving full ascension across all 42 lines costs approximately $2,000-$2,850 if purchased directly. Farming in-game takes years.

**Hero Skill Rerolling:**
- Reroll attempts cost gold (5-50 million per try) or gems
- Epic-quality skills for all four slots per hero: ~$500 per hero
- Perfect specialized heroes (like Tricksters for Polonia teams): $1,500+ per hero

**Estimated Total "Free-to-Play" Costs:**
- Ascension shards: $2,000-$3,000
- Event items: $2,000-$3,000
- Hero optimization: $2,000-$3,000
- Total realistic spending: $10,000+

## Gold-Making Strategies

1. Sigils: Craft or earn from events; sell for 2 million gold or 5 gems
2. Opulent Chests: Buy for ~17 gems, sell for ~17 million gold (1 million gold per gem return)
3. Precrafts: Sell at 10x base price or for gems
4. Champion Requests: Tier 6 items; high/max prices or gems
5. Superior Items: Premium due to collection book requirements
6. King Sales: Expensive quality items worth 5-10x base value; Limited Edition tokens increase to 10x

## Player Progression Issues

**Exponential Growth Problem:** Progression starts manageable (1, 2, 4, 8...) then becomes brutal (16,777,216, 33,554,432..., up to 1e9+). Players commonly hit walls and quit.

**Merchant Level Scaling:**
- Levels 1-30: Fast progression
- Level 39-40: 5 million experience points (noticeable jump)
- Level 59-60: 136 million points
- Level 98-99: 88 billion points
- Highest observed player: Level 84 (572 billion personal value)

**Time Investment:**
- Early game: Hours of play feel productive
- Mid-game: Weeks of grind for single levels
- Late game: Months or years per level without payment

## Critical Design Issues

**Guild Recruitment Crisis:** The game requires 20 active guild members but provides zero recruitment tools. Players resort to spamming world chat. "That the developers leave players to Discord and external sites to find players is a disgrace for what is or should be a casual free to play game."

**Hero Skill System Exploitation:** Skills are intentionally difficult to obtain ("designed to fall"). Reroll success rates are artificially low to encourage gem purchases. The author calls this "designed frustration."

**Investment Loss Mechanic:** When players leave, all their city investments downgrade the guild. Top guilds lose weeks/months/years of progress per departing player, creating recruitment desperation.

**Locked Buildings:** Exclusive workers cost $20-$30 each, pricing that "deters from the game for free to play gamers."

## Recommendations for Players

**If Playing Casually (Short-Term):** Considered acceptable; the game is graphically appealing and bug-free.

**If Playing Seriously (Long-Term):** Explicitly avoided. Recommends staying at low levels longer to: farm Bounty Bags more easily; upgrade energy/bins at sustainable pace; prepare properly before Tower of Titans difficulty scaling.

**Critical Mistake:** Leveling merchant level too quickly locks players into content difficulty mismatches before heroes are ready.

## Author Suggestions to Developers

1. Add warehouse for bins (separate from basement)
2. Implement guild recruitment matching by level/investments
3. Display auction worth alongside net worth (expose leeching players)
4. Allow two simultaneous upgrades (ideally via Royal Merchant subscription)
5. Add Small Talk bonus to subscription
6. Enable gem purchases for guild renown
7. Implement "inactive player buffer" system
8. Allow seed recovery from heroes/champions
9. Add King's Caprice bonus for ascended lines

Developer Jonathan acknowledged the buffer and gem-for-renown ideas favorably, but developers cite "limited time and resources" for most requests.

## Final Assessment

The guide characterizes Shop Titans as technically well-designed (graphics, interface, stability) but fundamentally predatory in business model — engineered to create frustration through artificial time gates and RNG mechanics, then monetize player impatience. Core conclusion: viable only for casual dippers with zero progression ambitions, dangerous for anyone seeking meaningful advancement.
