Snapshot

What kind of game this is

A shopkeeping + crafting management sim with an idle/clicker rhythm and a hero-collection RPG bolted on. Craft gear, sell it to heroes, send heroes to farm the materials for better gear. yt-05 yt-01

  • Lane: craft-shop tycoon, cozy surface, whale-driven economy underneath. assumed
  • Scale: ~105k downloads & ~$7.0M IAP revenue in 12 months; ~48k MAU. Small audience, extreme monetization. market-downloads-revenue market-active-users
  • Shape: mature 7-year live game, revenue flat-to-stable, near-zero paid user acquisition. market-downloads-revenue market-acquisition

The Loop

Craft → sell → quest → craft

Tap Craft (a short timer), sell the item to a customer at the counter, bank gold; crit-crafts throw a “MASTERPIECE!” popup with an Upgrade to EPIC button. Timers run 2–3s early, ~35 min at Tier 6. Selling spends Energy, regained through customer chatter, discounts, décor and pets. yt-01 yt-05 yt-03

Top players frame it as five things kept always-running: craft (“slots always full and spinning”), upgrade, rush (spend energy to refill), sell, quest. web-02

Selling is its own mini-game

A ~75% Small Talk roll refills energy; Surcharge sells above base price for a slice of missing energy. Full throughput needs three caps aligned — shop size (max 12 customers / 9 expansions), counter level (5 → 8 → 12), and 90–105 display items. web-02

“You craft items, you sell them, then you do it all over again… a fancy clicker. Relaxing. Cozy.” yt-05

Progression & Meta

Seven systems feeding one triangle

Shop expansion + furniture (racks = energy/selling, bins = crafting), blueprint/tier mastery, workers, a hero roster, Ascension specialization, guilds with shared town buildings, and seasons. The spine: crafting needs materials → materials come from hero quests → heroes need your crafted gear to clear quests → loot funds more crafting. Gear also wears out and must be repaired — the endgame's biggest gold sink. yt-02 yt-03 yt-05 web-10

Tier gating — the pacing skeleton

  • Shop equipment tiers unlock by merchant level: T2 @ L7 → T15 @ L65. web-02
  • Hero gear tiers unlock by hero level: T2 @ L3 → T15 @ L38. web-02
  • Ascension: first push 15 stars in every line (~165 shards/line) for a 10% surcharge-energy cut, then specialize to 51 (titan bonuses) or 21 (multicraft). web-02

Power curve: fast early (shop L1→3 in ~12 min), tiers 4–5 quick to master, then a widely-reported paywall around L20+ and again ~L50 as repair costs outrun income. Endgame flips from “craft to progress” to “optimize the selling machine.” yt-01 yt-02 web-09 web-10 yt-03

Monetization

The subscription is the fulcrum

F2P + IAP + a monthly subscription; negligible ads. The community-named “Royal Merchant” (~$10/mo) lets you repair gear with gold and grants event rewards — without it, post-L50 repair costs make the game “unfun.” One player: a subbed account earned x30 a free one. market-iap web-10

Gem-sink psychology

Anchor rule pushed by top players: 1 gem ≈ 10,000 gold, reframing every price as a bargain. Best sinks collapse a multi-day timer + gold cost + level gate at once:

  • Shop expansion: 2,000 gems ≈ 100M gold / L50 / 1d23h → 4,000 gems ≈ 500M gold / L75 / 4d23h. yt-04
  • Crafting slots: 1,000 → 2,500 gems each. yt-04
  • 50-gem “Magic Unlock” chest beats the 150-gem one on EV — trains cheap-tier repeat spend. yt-04
  • $100 ≈ 18,000 gems; starter bundles “totally worth buying.” yt-04

Currency sprawl — gold, gems, spin tickets, champion coins, guild coins, renown, fortune tokens, ascension shards — critics call it deliberately confusing (“Confusion Coins”). An always-on gem + button and an in-world king (Reinhold) pitch limited-time offers. yt-05 yt-01

Retention

The hooks that bring players back

Daily & time-gated

  • Timed collection reward (a 10:23h countdown seeded in the FTUE), daily boss, log-in-and-queue-8-crafts. yt-01 yt-02
  • Craft timers (to ~35 min), furniture-upgrade timers (guild-assist + gems), the gear-repair economy. yt-04 yt-03

Social & competitive

  • Guild town buildings — the shown high-level guild ran +25% craft / +50% resource / +51% worker. yt-02
  • Global + national + guild leaderboards drive spend-to-rank competition. web-10

Named live-ops: Tower of Titans (solo, monthly 2nd–30th, Titan's Soul reward); guild events King's Caprice, Dragon Invasion, Lost City of Gold (L25+); real-world Full Moon fusion windows. web-02

First Week → Day 30

The onboarding, walked

~L4: guilds + chat unlock (own guild costs 500 gems ≈ $5). D2–7: the craft→sell→quest triangle opens; “wash, rinse, repeat” sets in; monetization pressure builds ~L20+. yt-05 web-09

What Players Say

Loved and resented, in the same breath

Reviews are sharply bimodal — long-tenured fans versus a pay-to-win revolt. Android Play listing sits at 3.95★ with 94,846 five-stars against 25,314 one-stars; Metacritic reads 42 (“Generally Unfavorable”). review-playstore web-09 market-reviews

Praise

Cozy, relaxing; “complexity beyond casual”; genuinely playable F2P with patience + a good guild; deeply loyal veterans. yt-05 web-09

Complaints

Aggressive monetization (“puts up obstacles and sells you the solution”), repair-cost wall, currency bloat, paywalled friend-play, offer spam, thin story. web-09 web-10 yt-05

So What — For Sword Forge

What to steal, what to avoid

  • Steal the interlock. The craft↔hero↔guild triangle is why 48k players pay $7M — every login has a next upgrade. Elite D30 (16%) is the engine, not install volume. yt-02 market-retention
  • Steal the crit-craft juice. The MASTERPIECE popup + rarity multipliers (1×→5×) turn a routine craft into a slot-machine pull — directly applicable to Sword Forge's quality overlays. yt-01 web-02
  • Steal the cozy-diegetic tutorial. Mentor dialogue teaching one atomic action at a time, no modal walls. yt-01
  • Avoid the currency sprawl & repair wall. The same aggression that prints money caps the audience and drives the ~L50 churn cliff. yt-05 web-10 assumed
